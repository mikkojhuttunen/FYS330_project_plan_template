\chapter{Budget}

\instructionbox{List the expected costs of the project: materials,
consumables, equipment or software (including licenses), travel, and any
other significant expense. A short table (item, quantity, estimated cost)
works well if you have several items; a paragraph is enough if the budget
is simple or still uncertain. If costs are not yet known at the time of
writing, say so explicitly and state when/how the estimate will be
finalised (e.g.\ after a meeting with a collaborating lab).}
